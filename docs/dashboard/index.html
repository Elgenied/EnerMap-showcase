EnerMap · Open-source local energy assessment
ABOUT ENERMAP

Local energy. In perspective.

Explore energy demand, decarbonisation opportunities and alternative investment priorities through maps and scenario comparisons.

Guildford is the current application. The EnerMap workflow can be applied elsewhere with local building, household and energy inputs; this public explorer currently contains the Guildford results only.

Three assessment areas

  • Borough demand: 57,300 modelled dwellings and 84 LSOAs.
  • Planning scenarios: 28,663 dwellings across ten wards.
  • Network screening: 28,662 assigned dwellings in 271 inferred secondary-substation cells.

Reading the results

U0–U5 are fixed intervention scenarios. Financial results retain the study's fuel prices, carbon factors and incentive assumptions. Annual financial PV accounts use a 45% self-consumption assumption; the network view uses hourly PV coincidence. A 4 kWp cap is applied per dwelling in the PV scenarios.

Substation cells approximate supply areas using proximity. Ratios compare residential import or export peaks with the screening rating (reported ONAN capacity × 0.95); they are not an engineering assessment of available network capacity.

Deprivation and fuel poverty describe neighbourhoods. The construction-difficulty flag covers solid-brick or stone walls, oil/LPG/solid-fuel heating, pre-1900 homes or caravan accommodation. It does not establish that an individual home cannot be retrofitted.

Carbon-first and equity-first use the same U5 packages and total net budget. Equity weights combine deprivation, fuel-poverty prevalence, rented tenure and construction difficulty. Results show a priority order, not predicted household uptake.
